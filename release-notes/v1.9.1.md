## New

- **Sort the games of a day.** The Games section on the day page has a sort button: order logged, **game mode** (the same order as the tracked matches: ranked 1s, 2s, 3s, then casual, Honor Duels, private matches, then your own modes), account, most games or most wins.
- **Amount items on the in-game overlay.** A routine item counted in amounts (e.g. 50 shots) can now go on the overlay: it shows the amount and a big ✓ button (the done shortcut, Ctrl + Alt + O, works too). Press it when you're done and the routine goes on to the next item, so a whole routine can be run from inside the game. Auto-next puts amount items on the overlay by itself, and each amount item has an overlay button in the routine.

## Changed

- **Auto-next sets the next item up, without starting it.** When an item of a routine finishes, the next one is ready but waits for you: it looks different (dashed outline, dimmed time, **READY · PRESS ▶** on the overlay) until you start it with ▶ or the pause shortcut (Ctrl + Alt + P), then it looks normal. The sound still plays when the previous timer ends.
- **Finished timers wait for you.** A timer outside a routine, or the last one of a routine, no longer disappears when it runs out: it stays at 0:00 and flashes green on the overlay, in the top bar and on the day page until you press ✓ (or Done in the app), which logs it. The sound plays when the time is up. Starting another timer logs a finished one first, so it's never lost.
- **Paused timers pulse on the overlay** (amber, slower than a finished timer), so it's clear the clock isn't running.
