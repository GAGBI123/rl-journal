## Changed

- **The latest games at the top.** When you open an entry's games (e.g. Ranked 2s · 5 games), the last game you played is at the top; the numbers still count from your first game (#1). Training sessions are listed the same way, newest first.
- **"Most recent" sort for the Games section.** The default sort (formerly "Order logged") now puts the mode you played last at the top, and a mode moves up when you play it again.
