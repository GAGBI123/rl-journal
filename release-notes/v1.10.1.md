## Changed

- **Only the time on games and sessions.** Games played after midnight (while the day is still open) no longer show their date in an entry's list of games, just the time.
- **Forfeit instead of "Left early".** A game you left before the end now shows as Forfeit, in the games list, Tracked matches, Accounts and notifications.
- **Games are deleted from their entry.** Tracked matches no longer has a delete button: open the games of an entry in the Games section (e.g. Ranked 2s · 5 games) and delete a game there.
