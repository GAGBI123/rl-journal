## New

- **Season recap.** A completed season now opens with its recap: training time, games and win rate, ranked 1v1 / 2v2 / 3v3 games with wins and losses, casual, Honor Duels and other modes, what you trained most, your best training day, most games in a day, longest streak, goals / assists / saves per game, shooting %, your best match and games per account.
- **Export seasons as text files.** **Export .txt** on a season saves it as a text file you can open in Notepad, and **Settings → Your data → Export seasons as text** saves every season at once, one file each. Every day is written like a training log (what you trained and for how long, total training time, 1vs1 / 2vs2 / 3vs3 matches), with the season's totals at the end.
- **Sort your tracked matches.** The sort button on a day's tracked matches: most recent (default), oldest first, by game mode (Ranked 1s, 2s, 3s, then casual, then Honor Duels and private matches, newest first inside each), by account, by result or by most points. The table now shows your points too.
- **Games per account.** Games you play on different accounts get their own entry on the day, in that account's color (e.g. *Ranked 2s · Main* and *Ranked 2s · Alt*). **Merge** puts them together in one entry; click its accounts badge to see how many games each account played, or **Split** them again. Prefer them together all the time? Turn off **Keep each account's games apart** in Settings.
- **Private matches out of your record.** **Settings → Private matches**: *In record* (like before), *Not in record* (still logged as games, but left out of your wins, losses and win rates, also for the ones already logged) or *Don't log*.
