## Fixed

- **Routines could double your training time.** If you trained something first (for example with a timer) and then ticked the same item off in a routine, its time was added again, so 15m became 30m. Routines now always go by what your day really has: anything logged for an activity, before or after you added the routine, counts towards its item, and ticking an item only adds what's still missing. Ticking something you already did adds nothing.
- A finished timer can never be logged twice.

If one of your days got doubled times in 1.4 or 1.5.0, fix them with the pencil next to the entry on that day.
