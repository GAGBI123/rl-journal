## What's new

- **Stopwatch.** Next to **Timer** there's now **Stopwatch**: it counts up from 0 while you train, and when you stop it the time is added to the day. Stop it from the top bar, the ✓ on the in-game overlay, or **Ctrl + Alt + O** (works in game).
- **Days stay in place.** Days in the middle of a season can't be deleted anymore. Clearing a day (eraser icon) empties what's logged on it but keeps the day.
- **Add days with +.** The "Add days" button is gone. A **+** tile after the last day adds one day. Only the last day can be removed.
- **Closing a season removes the remaining days** after today by itself (days you logged something on are kept).
