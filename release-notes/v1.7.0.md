## New

- **A new look: Aurora.** Glass panels over a glowing background, gradient accents, new fonts and bolder numbers. It's the new default; the original look is still there as **Classic** in **Settings → Appearance**.
- **Your own colors.** Pick a color set (Purple haze, Toxic, Champion gold, Ice, Crimson, Mono…) or choose the accent, background, cards, text, wins and losses yourself. Works with both looks.
- **A new app icon, or your own.** RL Journal has a new icon, and **Settings → Appearance → App icon** lets you use any picture instead: the window, the taskbar, the tray and your RL Journal shortcuts use it.
- **Delete single tracked games.** Each game in a day's **Tracked matches** list has a delete button, for games that weren't yours or were cancelled. That day's game count and win/loss go down with it.
- **About page**, with the story of who came up with RL Journal: **Ascènd.**

## Changed

- **Private matches are logged by default.** Turn on **Ignore private matches** in Settings to skip them again.

## Fixed

- **Cancelled matches were logged as games.** A match that's called off before kickoff (for example because a player didn't join) is no longer logged: with no touches, no goals and no time on the clock, it isn't a game. Already logged ones are marked *Didn't start?* in the day's tracked matches, so you can delete them.
- **Overtime wins showed a tied score** (like a 2–2 win). The overtime goal is counted now and the game is marked OT.
