## New

- **Settings like a game's settings menu.** Tabs across the top: Appearance, Sections & activities, Games & logging, Timer & overlay, App & updates and Your data, one group of settings at a time. The left and right arrow keys switch tabs, and Settings opens on the tab you were last on.
- **Show times in days, or only hours and minutes.** **Settings → Appearance → Times**: *Days, hours, minutes* (like before, e.g. 1d 4h 20m) or *Hours and minutes* (28h 20m). It applies everywhere, exported text files included.
- **Completed seasons next to the current one.** On the Seasons page your completed seasons now sit right next to the season you're playing, newest first, instead of below the key stats. With many seasons, the newest are shown and the rest are one click away.

## Changed

- **Text export, closer to the Notepad log.** Days are written by number only (day 1, day 2, …, no dates), every training activity is listed on every day with **x** when you didn't do it that day, and the 1vs1 / 2vs2 / 3vs3 lines show x instead of 0. The season totals at the end refer to days by number too.

## Fixed

- **Linux: the timer overlay now shows over the game.** Wayland desktops (KDE, GNOME, …) don't let an app keep a window on top of others, so the overlay never appeared over Rocket League. RL Journal now runs through XWayland there (like the game does under Proton), where the overlay stays above the game in Borderless and Fullscreen. Set `RLJ_WAYLAND=1` to keep the native Wayland window.
