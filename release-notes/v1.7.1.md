## Fixed

- **Game scores were far too high in 1.7.0** (like a 26–14 loss). Rocket League sends a goal again when it shows the goal replay, and 1.7.0 counted those as new goals. Replayed goals aren't counted now, so scores match the scoreboard again. Wins and losses were always right.
- **Scores of games logged by 1.7.0 are removed**, since the right score can't be worked out afterwards. The games themselves, their wins, losses and your stats stay.
- A match the game calls a **no contest** isn't logged as a game.
