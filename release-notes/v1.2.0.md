## What's new

- **Sections inside sections.** Put *Freeplay* inside Training and log *Overall*, *Flicks* and so on inside it. Statistics show the total for each section and the split inside it. An activity you already log can be turned into a section, and its history moves to *Overall*.
- **Training timer.** Pick an activity, enter a time and click **Start timer**. When it ends it's added to the day by itself. Pause, finish early or cancel it from the top bar.
- **In-game overlay.** While Rocket League runs, a small timer shows at the top of the screen with a pause button. Use Borderless or Windowed mode. Turn it off or move it in Settings.
- Option to turn off automatic update checks.
- RL Journal is now open source under the MIT license.
