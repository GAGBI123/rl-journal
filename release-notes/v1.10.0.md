## New

- **Every game of a day, one by one.** A game entry on the day page (e.g. Ranked 2s · 5 games) has a **5 games** button that opens the list of those games in the order you played them: the result and score (with OT), your points, goals / assists / saves / shots, the time, and the account when you played on more than one. Give any game a note of its own with the note button (Enter saves). Games with a note show a note icon in Tracked matches too.
- **Training in sessions.** Each time you add to an activity on a day (a timer, a stopwatch, a routine item or by hand) is kept as its own session, and the day still shows the total: 5 minutes of dribbling and then 10 more is 15m. Open **2 sessions** on the entry to see each one with its time and where it came from, give each its own note, change its minutes (or amount), move it to another day or delete it. Moving an entry lets you pick which sessions go.

## Changed

- An activity logged several times on a day keeps a note per session instead of one joined description. To change those notes, open the entry's sessions; the Edit window changes the total (the difference goes on the latest session). Entries logged before this update count as one session.
