## New

- **You end your days.** Games are now logged to the current day until you press **End day** (in the sidebar or at the top of the day), so a session that goes past midnight still counts for the day you started it. Pressing it means you're done: the next games go to the next day. Ended a day by mistake? Press **Reopen day**. If you forget, a day that's been open for a whole extra day ends by itself. This is on by default; turn off **End days yourself** in Settings to have a new day start at midnight like before.
- **Move entries to another day.** The new arrows button next to an entry moves it to any other day, all of it or just part: some of the minutes, a number of games, or exactly which tracked games (for example the ones you played after midnight). Their match details move along.

## Fixed

- **Watching a friend's Honor Duel logged it as yours.** When two others duel in your lobby, the game's camera follows one of them, and the app took that player for you: it logged their result as your win or loss and added them as a new account. Duels you only watch are no longer logged, and a player who isn't one of your accounts is never added from an Honor Duel.

If a watched duel was logged for you: remove that player's account in **Accounts & Sync**, and delete or fix the Honor Duel entry on that day.
