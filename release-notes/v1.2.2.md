## What's new

- **Hide or show a day's record.** Each day has a **Hide record** button that hides that day's wins and losses (summary, entries and match results). It's saved for that day only; click **Show record** to bring it back.
- **Pause the timer in game:** press **Ctrl + Alt + P** to pause or resume, even while Rocket League has focus (the game keeps the mouse to itself, so clicks could miss the overlay). Pick another key or turn it off in Settings.
- The overlay's pause button now reacts on press and shows the shortcut.
